Family lineage شجرہ نسب

Descendants of Baba Phula

Baba Phula came from Kyaan, Iran, some three to four hundred years ago and settled in Bassali, where the government of the day allotted him 80 acres. His six sons spread out to the nearby villages. The family later married into the Abbasi family, who had moved from Murree to the Gujar Khan area.
